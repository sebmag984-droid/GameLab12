
Entrance Hall
You stand inside the entrance hall of Blackwood Mansion. A grand staircase rises before you, and an old chandelier hangs from the ceiling. Dust covers the marble floor, and the front doors have locked behind you. A cold breeze moves through the hall even though every window is shut.
#
Living Room
The living room is filled with faded furniture and torn curtains. An enormous fireplace stands against the far wall, surrounded by portraits of people whose faces have been scratched out. A layer of dust covers a small table, but one drawer appears to have been opened recently.
#
Library
The mansion library contains hundreds of old books stacked on tall wooden shelves. A desk sits beneath a flickering lamp, and scattered papers cover its surface. One book has been pulled halfway out of the shelf, revealing a narrow space behind it.
#
Kitchen
The kitchen looks as though its last meal was abandoned years ago. Rusted pots hang above a large stove, and cracked plates cover the counters. A pantry door stands slightly open, and the air smells faintly of smoke and old wood.
#
Bedroom
This bedroom belonged to one of the mansion's former residents. A large four-poster bed stands beneath a torn canopy, and an antique dresser rests against the wall. A locked drawer sits beneath a mirror, while an old keyhole catches the light.
#
Attic
You climb into the attic, where broken furniture and dusty trunks fill the narrow space. Strange marks cover the wooden beams, and the wind whistles through a crack in the roof. One trunk has a rusty lock, and something inside shifts when the floor creaks.
#
Basement
The basement is cold, damp, and almost completely dark. Stone walls surround a collection of old crates and empty shelves. A weak light shines beneath a heavy wooden door at the end of the corridor. The air grows colder as you approach it.
#
Study
The study contains a large desk, a damaged typewriter, and a wall covered with old documents. A portrait hangs crookedly above the fireplace. Several papers mention the Blackwood family, but one page has been torn out of a journal.
